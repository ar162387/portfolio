## The voice and production style

Warm, witty, English, face plus demonstrations. Talk to an owner who is busy with work, rather than an agency founder shopping for a tech stack. The first line earns attention; the screen supplies the evidence. Use ordinary words like call, calendar, customer and invoice.

The tone is informed by our conversation and your stated preference. It has not been matched against three transcribed speaking samples. Read each script aloud once and replace any phrase you would not naturally say.

Use the website's cream, charcoal and warm orange on covers. Mix founder frames with genuine screens; avoid a grid of identical text tiles. A cover gets one short idea. In 1080 × 1920 Reels keep important text below y=230, above y=1440 and away from the right-hand action controls. Burn in readable subtitles. Frame changes should follow the explanation, rather than interrupting useful evidence.

The three recurring series are **After the Beep**, **Call My Bluff**, and **Then What?** They organize your content; they are not three additional products.
