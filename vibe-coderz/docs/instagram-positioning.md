## A truthful demo enquiry reply

```text
I have a working receptionist demo you can hear. What kind of business do you run, and do you want it to take enquiries or book appointments?
```

Once the prospect answers, explain what can be adapted and confirm their existing tools. Say their tailored demo is ready after testing it. Reusing the agent saves build effort, but the business information and integrations still need validation.

## Campaign focus

The website can serve all three countries while individual campaigns use relevant business examples and one recognizable call-handling problem. Keep the overall brand open across both market families. Campaign segmentation is a learning method, not a requirement to adopt a permanent micro-niche. Do not combine all 40 business types in an outreach message.

A first **test hypothesis** could be a small group of HVAC or plumbing businesses, because their call-handling scenario is easy to demonstrate and those examples appear in the voice-agent video. This is not evidence that they outperform other niches or that the USA should be chosen over the UK or Australia.

Compare candidate markets using access to owners, observed need, compatible tools, ability to deliver the demo and feedback from conversations. Keep the same offer within a test so replies are interpretable. A clinic campaign and a contractor campaign should each use their own relevant example.
